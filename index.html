Display & behaviour

Paths

Paths practices a short phrase one word at a time. Choice buttons use words from this board (emoji, colour, level). Stories are still in the app, but the stories tab is not on the tab bar. Expanded keeps the shorter menu. Sentences and Full add I can, put it on / in, please / thank you, and I like …ing.

Off: a tap is quiet, and the whole phrase is spoken at the last word (Say whole still speaks whenever you tap it).

Off: → words stays in its place and does nothing, so the other buttons do not move.

Open slots (food, places, actions) use this many buttons. A fixed pair, such as open / close, stays as written.

Uses the usage log on this iPad. Core words stay in their usual order. When the log is off, nothing changes.

Board, private content and device backup

All words (starter, middle and full) and the stories are built into the app and update with it; no import is needed. Family photos/stories come from the private copy of the app. Photos or recordings you add here are stored only on this device; “Export” saves a backup of them. Importing a pack still works (it adds photos/recordings/stories on top).

Words: show / hide and photos

Tapping a word below will:

Hidden words leave a blank cell; nothing else moves. Tapping a word to hide or show it switches Words shown to Custom, starting from the level you were on. L1, L2 … = the word's level (1 starter, 2 middle, 3 expanded, 4 sentences, 5 conversation). Photos come from the camera or photo library, are shrunk to 640 px and kept only on this device (they go into the pack backup when you export). Empty cells show their row/column for adding words to a pack file.

Stories: photos

Pages are never read aloud automatically. Adding a photo to a sample story copies that story into your pack.

Usage log (this iPad only)

Off by default. When on, stores only the time and the word, in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
